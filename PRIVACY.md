# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 28, 2026**

Twitch Player Reset does not collect, sell, or send personal information to the extension publisher. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or publisher-operated servers.

The extension stores:

- The spinner reset delay in the browser's extension sync storage. The browser may synchronize this setting between the user's signed-in browser installations according to the browser's sync settings.
- The count of successful player resets, and the channel login and display name that count belongs to, in local extension storage on the current browser profile. The count increases only after the player has been playing and is then reset. It returns to zero when a different channel page loads. The channel login and display name are stored so those page loads can be told apart and the popup can show the name as Twitch displays it.

This information is used only to provide the extension's settings and counter. The browser may synchronize the reset delay using the signed-in browser profile's sync service, subject to the browser's own settings and privacy policy. The extension does not send this information to the extension publisher. Removing the extension removes its stored extension data according to the browser's normal behavior.

The extension runs on Twitch pages to detect the player loading spinner and request a player reset. Twitch's own privacy practices continue to apply to Twitch page activity.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the extension publisher through the support contact listed on the extension's store listing.
